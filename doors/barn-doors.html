What is a sliding barn door?

A sliding barn door glides along a top-mounted rail instead of swinging open. It saves floor space, makes a design statement, and works beautifully for closets, pantries, bathrooms, and room dividers.

We build every barn door by hand — flat-panel, chevron, X-brace, herringbone, or any custom pattern. Painted, stained, or natural. Sized to your opening, not a catalog SKU.

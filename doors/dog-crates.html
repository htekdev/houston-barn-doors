Custom Dog Furniture Crates

Style meets comfort — crates that double as beautiful furniture.

What are dog furniture crates?

These are handcrafted wooden dog crates that double as beautiful furniture. End tables, credenzas, built-ins — designed to match your home's style and finish, not look like a cage.

Same solid-wood craftsmanship as our doors. Custom sized for your dog, designed for your space. Real ventilation, secure latches, comfortable for hours.

✓

Furniture-grade

✓

Built to your dog's dimensions — small, medium, large, or extra-large.

✓

Designed for your space

End table, credenza, under-counter, or freestanding. Match your existing furniture finish.

✓

Ventilation & safety

Proper airflow with slatted or mesh panels. Secure latches. No wire cage look.

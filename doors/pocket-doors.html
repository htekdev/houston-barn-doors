What is a pocket door?

A pocket door slides completely into the wall — when open, there's no door visible at all. Just a clean opening. No swing radius eating into your floor plan.

Perfect for tight bathrooms, closets, pantries, laundry rooms, and anywhere a swinging door doesn't fit your life. We handle the framing, hardware, and install — not just the door.

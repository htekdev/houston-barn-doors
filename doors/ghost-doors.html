Concealed Ghost Doors

Architecture, not hardware — the door that reads as part of the wall.

What is a concealed ghost door?

A ghost door reads as part of the wall until you touch it. There's no visible frame, no visible track, no hardware on the surface — just a clean line where a door used to be.

The concealed mechanism hides inside the wall, creating a seamless transition from wall to door. When closed, guests can't tell where the wall ends and the door begins.

It's the quietest way to make a room feel bigger, more modern, and unmistakably custom.
